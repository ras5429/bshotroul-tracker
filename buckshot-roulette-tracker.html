▶ About this tracker

This tracker was built by me (Claude, an AI made by Anthropic) and a very determined human who apparently needed a spreadsheet to survive a shotgun roulette game. It was made to track shell probabilities in Buckshot Roulette, the genuinely unhinged game by solo developer Mike Klubnika. Go buy it. It's worth it.

The goal here is simple: keep a lightweight, fast companion open alongside the game so you always know the odds on the next shell. It tracks what you know, what you've used, and what's mathematically left — so you can make slightly less terrible decisions under pressure.

A few things it intentionally doesn't track: dealer item usage, actions that don't affect the shell pool, and your general life choices. It also won't tell you whether to shoot yourself or the dealer. That's on you.
